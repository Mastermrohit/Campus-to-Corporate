Slide 02 • Problem Context

Problem Statement & Executive Summary

Acute gap between academic curricula and corporate industry expectations in higher education.

48%+ Unemployability Rate

Graduates in tier-2 & tier-3 districts of MP lack awareness of modern industry skills like System Design, DevOps, and Microservices.

1:1,000 Counselor Ratio

College Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) lack scalable software to provide personalized 1:1 career roadmaps for thousands of students.

Missed Public Schemes

Students miss official government apprenticeships (NATS/NAPS) and MP Rojgar opportunities due to decentralized notification channels.

The Campus to Corporate Solution

An end-to-end AI platform that ingests student academic transcripts, diagnoses skill gaps via interactive radar geometry, generates 12-week roadmaps, rewrites ATS resumes into STAR format, conducts AI voice interviews, and matches state/central job schemes.

Slide 04 • Technical Approach

Technical Architecture & Stack

Microservices architecture powered by React 19, FastAPI/Node, Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, and JWT security.

Technology Stack
  • Frontend UI: React 19 + Vite + Tailwind v4
  • Charts Engine: Recharts SVG Radar
  • AI Intelligence: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash
  • Auth & Security: RFC 7519 JWT (HS256)
  • Data Storage: Firebase Firestore / Supabase
